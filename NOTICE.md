# Provenance and reuse

This repository contains coursework originally completed at the University of
Texas at San Antonio. Original comments identify supplied scaffolding where
known. Team contributions and supplied datasets remain subject to their original
authors' rights. No permission to redistribute original course instructions,
third-party datasets, or team material is inferred from publication here.

Original files have no verified blanket reuse license. Obtain permission from
the relevant rights holders before reusing them outside permitted educational use.
The root LICENSE expressly covers only new maintenance additions.

Synthetic demo inputs supplied during maintenance are clearly documented in
DEVELOPMENT.md. They are replacements for running examples, not recovered course
submissions or factual records. They may be reused under the maintenance MIT license.

Restored Gradle wrapper JARs, where present, come from the corresponding official
Gradle release and retain Gradle's Apache-2.0 terms. Their SHA-256 values were
verified against the official release checksums. Other dependencies retain their
own licenses; dependency manifests do not change those licenses.
